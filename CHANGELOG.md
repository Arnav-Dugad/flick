# Changelog

Release notes for each version. The release workflow copies the section matching the tag into
the GitHub Release (and Flick's update sheet shows it).

## [0.4.1] - 2026-11-09

First public release. (The v0.4.0 tag was a build that never published.)

- **37 hands-free gestures**: head tilt, turn and nod; long blink, double blink, smile, open mouth and raised eyebrows; open palm, fist, thumbs up/down, victory, point up and air swipes; phone tilt, shake and double/triple knock on the back; volume buttons; and experimental offline voice words.
- **Per-app profiles** with smart defaults for Reels & Shorts, YouTube, browsers, and books & PDFs. The camera only runs inside apps you've picked.
- **Live calibration** for every gesture: a signal meter, tracking dots, and sensitivity, hold and cooldown sliders.
- **Actions**: swipes, scrolling, taps, double-tap to like, long-press, back, home, recents, notifications, screenshot, lock screen, split screen, and media and volume controls.
- **Automatic updates** from GitHub Releases, verified by checksum and signing certificate before installing.
- Material 3 design with dynamic colour, a dark theme and a pure-black option.
- Settings → About links to every release and its changelog.
